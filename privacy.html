🛒 Deal Monitor Bot — Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Deal Monitor Bot ("the Bot") handles information. We are committed to your privacy — the Bot does not collect any personal information about users.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Bot does not collect, store, log, or share any of the following:

  • Discord usernames, user IDs, or profile information
  • Message content or command history
  • Server names, server IDs, or membership information
  • IP addresses or device information
  • Any personally identifiable information

2. What the Bot Does Store

The Bot maintains a local SQLite database solely to track which deals have already been posted, preventing duplicate notifications. This database contains only deal identifiers (such as product URLs and titles) sourced from third-party websites. It contains no user data of any kind.

3. How the Bot Works

On a configurable interval, the Bot automatically checks Amazon, CamelCamelCamel, and Slickdeals for gaming deals that meet a minimum discount threshold. Qualifying deals are posted to the configured Discord channel. No user interaction is required or logged.

4. Third-Party Services

The Bot interacts with the following third-party services to function:

  • Amazon — product pricing and deal data
  • CamelCamelCamel — price history RSS feeds
  • Slickdeals — community deal RSS feeds
  • Railway — hosting infrastructure

These services may collect their own data per their respective privacy policies. The Bot does not pass any user information to these services.

5. Data Retention

The deal-tracking database is stored ephemerally on the hosting server and is cleared on each redeployment. No user data is ever retained.

6. Children's Privacy

The Bot does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has used the Bot in violation of this policy, please contact us.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions or concerns, please open an issue on the GitHub repository.
